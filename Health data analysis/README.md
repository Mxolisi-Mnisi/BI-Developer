🏥 National Health Report – Power BI Dashboard

This interactive Power BI dashboard delivers a comprehensive National Health Report, offering deep insights into patient trends, healthcare costs, and treatment outcomes.

📊 Key Features

Core KPIs: Admissions, doctor availability, bed capacity, billing, and length of stay with year-over-year comparisons.

Patient Segmentation: Filter by gender and age group to target specific populations.

Condition-Specific Analysis: Dive into focused visual summaries and test result trends for conditions such as Asthma.

Hospital & Operational Trends: Compare patient volumes and treatment outcomes across leading U.S. hospitals.

Financial & Operational Insights: Track revenue by hospital and insurance provider, along with cost and length-of-stay trends.

🛠 Interactivity

Dynamic filters by Year, Hospital, and Medical Condition for customizable exploration.

Clean, intuitive visuals designed for data-driven decision-making and healthcare resource planning.

🚀 Getting Started

Download the .pbix file from this repository.

Open with Power BI Desktop (latest version recommended).

Connect to your own data sources or explore the provided dataset.
